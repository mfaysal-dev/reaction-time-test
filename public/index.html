How the reaction time test works

  1. Tap the box. It turns red: wait.
  2. After a random 1.5 to 4.5 seconds it turns green. Tap at once.
  3. Tapping too early counts as a false start, and that try is repeated.
  4. After 5 tries you get your average in milliseconds, your best time and a rank.

What is a normal reaction time?

Most adults score between 200 and 300 ms on a visual test like this. The online median sits around 250–275 ms. Your device adds a little lag too: a 60 Hz screen can add up to 16 ms, and Bluetooth mice or TVs add more. Use a wired mouse and a high refresh rate screen for your best score.

Tips to improve your reflexes

  • Sleep well and test at the same time of day.
  • Keep your finger resting on the button or screen.
  • Look at the centre of the box, not the edges.
  • Fast-paced games, sport and caffeine in moderation can all help a little.

FAQs

What is a good reaction time?

Under 250 ms is better than most people. Under 200 ms is excellent.

Why is my score slower on my phone?

Touch screens and some browsers add 20–50 ms of delay. A wired mouse is usually faster.

Is my score saved?

Only your personal best, in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.

How fast are F1 drivers?

Elite drivers react to start lights in roughly 150–200 ms.
